How it works

1. List

Farmers upload residue quantity, quality and collection details.

2. Match

Rule-based matching checks fit, demand, net value and location.

3. Negotiate

Buyers send offers while farmers compare transport and handling costs.

4. Deliver

Orders move through collection, delivery and completion milestones.
